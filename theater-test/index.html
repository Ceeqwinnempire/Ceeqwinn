DISCORDANT DESCENDANTS

Chapter 2 — The Gathering

by Confidence Cyrus

THE NIGHT OF THE GATHERING

By nightfall, the Okoye residence had transformed.

Golden lights illuminated the long driveway. Luxury vehicles arrived one after another. Music drifted through the mansion.

Lexis stood at the staircase, watching the room below.

Her father stood nearby.

Then Lexis noticed her.

A mysterious woman wearing a dark emerald dress.

The woman did not look away.

Sirena was there too.

An elderly man eventually stepped forward.

The room gradually became silent.

He raised his voice.

THE ELDERLY MAN

It is time to announce the next heir.

THE ANNOUNCEMENT

A black envelope bearing the family crest was brought into the room.

Lexis stared at it.

Then the elderly man called her forward.

HER FATHER

There are two choices.

THE WOMAN IN GREEN

Lexis returned to the mansion.

She found her father near a private archive.

He finally admitted that the woman was someone from their family.

Her aunt had disappeared twenty years ago.

And now she was here.

HER FATHER

Your aunt disappeared twenty years ago.

THE KEY

Sirena entered the archive.

The moment she saw the object in Lexis's hand, her expression changed.

SIRENA

The door.

She explained that their grandfather had sealed something away long ago.

Then the lights went out.

THE HEIR HAS AWAKENED.

The crest on the key began to glow.

Somewhere nearby, something opened.
