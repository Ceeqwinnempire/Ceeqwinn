Prototype 0.1: This version provides manual region selection and controlled semantic changes. It does not yet automatically interpret sketches or redraw garments with AI. Unknown construction details remain unknown.

1. Sketch workshop

Original preserved

Uncolored drawings are welcome. You can work with a garment alone or a character wearing it.

Choose Mark region, then click or tap around the component. Mark at least three points and press Finish region.

Upload a sketch or try a sample selection.

3. Mutation review

Approval required
Draft a mutation to inspect the proposed change.

The preservation contract protects tier identity and order, silhouette, length, fabric, color, decoration, unrelated garment parts, character styling, the original sketch, and prior versions.

Approval changes the structured record only. It does not redraw the uploaded image.
