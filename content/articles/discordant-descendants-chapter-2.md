# DISCORDANT DESCENDANTS

*Chapter 2 — The Gathering*

<!--THEATRE:SCENE|morning-after|/content/images/A_beautiful_young_woman_full_b.jpeg-->

The morning after the first gathering felt strangely quiet.

Lexis woke with the uncomfortable feeling that something had changed, even though the room looked exactly as it had the night before.

She sat still for a moment.

The silence seemed to be waiting for her.

<!--THEATRE:SCENE|night-gathering|/content/images/Luxurious_royal_theatre_curtai (1).jpeg-->

## THE NIGHT OF THE GATHERING

Golden lights lined the enormous driveway.

The estate looked almost unreal beneath the evening sky.

Guests moved through the entrance in carefully chosen clothes while music drifted through the grounds.

Lexis stepped forward.

For a moment, the entire gathering seemed to hold its breath.

<!--THEATRE:BEAT|announcement|/content/images/Full-body_cinematic_portrait_o - 2025-10-30T110920.194.jpeg-->

Someone was about to make an announcement.

The conversations around Lexis slowly faded.

A strange feeling settled over her.

<!--THEATRE:DIALOGUE|A VOICE|Everyone, please come forward. There is something you need to know.-->

Lexis looked toward the front of the gathering.

Then the family stepped forward.

## THE ANNOUNCEMENT

The room became quiet.

Everyone waited.

Lexis knew she could no longer stand at the edge of the gathering and pretend that nothing concerned her.

[CHOICE:chapter2-theatre-choice-01]

[A] Walk forward and face the family.

[B] Leave the gathering before they can explain anything.

[CONSEQUENCE:A]

<!--THEATRE:BEAT|face-family|/content/images/Lexis_Azunna_ministry.jpg.jpeg-->

Lexis walked forward.

She kept her shoulders steady even though her thoughts were racing.

The family watched her approach.

[ENDCHOICE]

[CONSEQUENCE:B]

<!--THEATRE:BEAT|leave-gathering|/content/images/Lexis_Azunna_street.jpg.jpeg-->

Lexis turned away from the gathering.

She needed answers, but she needed them on her own terms.

[ENDCHOICE]

<!--THEATRE:SCENE|woman-in-green|/content/images/2D_cartoon_Disney_character_di (16).jpeg-->

## THE WOMAN IN GREEN

Later that evening, Lexis noticed a woman standing apart from everyone else.

Something about the woman in green made Lexis stop.

She looked familiar.

Too familiar.

[CHOICE:chapter2-theatre-choice-02]

[A] Ask Dad what he knows about her disappearance.

[B] Search the private archive before Dad can stop her.

[CONSEQUENCE:A]

<!--THEATRE:BEAT|ask-dad|/content/images/Lexis_Azunna_soft.jpg.jpeg-->

Lexis turned toward Dad.

“Who is she?”

Dad did not answer immediately.

[ENDCHOICE]

[CONSEQUENCE:B]

<!--THEATRE:BEAT|private-archive|/content/images/2D_cartoon_Disney_character_di (17).jpeg-->

Lexis slipped away toward the private archive.

If there was an answer, she suspected it was hidden somewhere inside.

[ENDCHOICE]

<!--THEATRE:SCENE|the-key|/content/images/2D_cartoon_Disney_character_di (2).jpeg-->

## THE KEY

Inside the archive, Lexis discovered something she had never seen before.

A small key rested inside an old case.

She reached toward it.

For the first time that night, she understood that the gathering had never been the real beginning.

It had only been the doorway.
