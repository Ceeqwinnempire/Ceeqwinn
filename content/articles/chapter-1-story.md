# The Gathering

[THEATRE]

## The Evening Begins

Lexis Azunna stood beside her father at the family gathering.

Golden lights filled the mansion. Guests whispered to one another, and an elderly man waited on the balcony.

Her father held her hand.

"Whatever happens tonight, Lexis, think carefully before you act."

Lexis looked at him.

"That sounds suspiciously like a warning."

[/THEATRE]

---

## Choice Test One

[CHOICE:chapter1-choice-01]

[A] Walk forward and demand an explanation.

[B] Stay beside Dad and observe the guests.

[CONSEQUENCE:A]

Lexis released her father's hand and walked toward the balcony.

"If everyone is waiting for me, somebody should explain why."

The elderly man opened a black envelope.

"You have asked the right question," he said. "But you may not like the answer."

Lexis decided that she would hear him out.

[ENDCHOICE]

[CONSEQUENCE:B]

Lexis stayed beside her father and watched the guests.

A woman in an emerald dress looked directly at her, then slipped through a side door.

Lexis quietly memorized the woman's face.

If nobody else would explain the mystery, she would investigate it herself.

[ENDCHOICE]

---

## Choice Test Two

Lexis now had another decision to make.

[CHOICE:chapter1-choice-02]

[A] Ask Dad about the black envelope.

[B] Follow the woman in emerald.

[CONSEQUENCE:A]

Lexis turned toward her father.

"Tell me what is inside that envelope."

Her father hesitated.

"It contains a family secret."

"That is not an answer," Lexis replied.

He promised to explain more, but only after the gathering.

Lexis decided to hold him to that promise.

[ENDCHOICE]

[CONSEQUENCE:B]

Lexis followed the corridor toward the side door.

The woman in emerald was waiting near a locked room.

"You are curious," the woman said.

"I have been told that before," Lexis replied.

The woman looked toward the door.

"Then perhaps you are finally ready to learn why your family is afraid."

[ENDCHOICE]
