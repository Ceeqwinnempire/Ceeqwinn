# The Gathering

*Chapter 1 — A New Beginning*

[THEATRE]



## The Evening Begins

The evening had barely begun, but Lexis Azunna already suspected that something was wrong.

Golden lights illuminated the entrance to the mansion. Guests arrived in elegant clothes, their voices blending with the soft music drifting through the hall.

Lexis stood beside her father, watching the guests.

"This is supposed to be a family gathering?" she whispered.

Her father adjusted his jacket.

"Yes."

She glanced at the enormous crowd.

"There are enough people here to form a small government."

He laughed.

"Please, Lexis. Just behave yourself tonight."

"I always behave."

Her father gave her a look.

She sighed.

"Fine. I usually behave."

## An Unexpected Announcement

The music suddenly stopped.

An elderly man appeared on the balcony above the gathering. Conversations faded as everyone turned towards him.

"Ladies and gentlemen," he announced, "we have an important matter to discuss."

Lexis looked at her father.

His expression had changed.

"Dad?"

"Listen," he whispered.

The elderly man raised a black envelope bearing an unfamiliar crest.

"Tonight, we must address the future of this family."

Lexis studied the envelope.

Something about that crest seemed familiar.

And she intended to discover why.

## A Question Without an Answer

"Who is that man?" Lexis asked.

Her father hesitated.

"Someone you should listen to."

"That isn't an answer."

"Lexis."

"What? You keep telling me to listen, but nobody is explaining anything."

Before he could respond, the elderly man called her name.

"Miss Lexis Azunna. Please come forward."

Every eye in the hall turned towards her.

Lexis slowly looked at her father.

He reached for her hand.

For the first time that evening, he looked afraid.
