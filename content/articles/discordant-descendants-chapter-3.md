# DISCORDANT DESCENDANTS

## CHAPTER 3 — THE CHOICE

The afternoon light had begun to soften across Emerald High.

Lexis stood quietly for a moment, thinking about what came next.

Tonight mattered.

Not because anyone had told her that it did.

Because something about the invitation felt different.

She looked down at her phone.

One decision remained.

**What should Lexis wear?**

## THE MOMENT

Lexis returned a little later, ready to leave.

She felt different somehow.

Not because she had changed who she was.

She had simply chosen how she wanted to face the evening.

Lexis read it carefully.

Whatever happened next, she would have to choose.
